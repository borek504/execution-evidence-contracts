# Glossary

## record

A candidate's untrusted report, which may contain observations and data from various sources.

## expected

Identities and policy established independently before execution, serving as a baseline for comparison.

## verifier_context

Separately supplied observations whose origin a real integration must establish, providing additional context for verification.

## attempt_id / unit_id

Identifiers used to match inputs to the intended attempt and unit, not authentication credentials, facilitating traceability and consistency.

## Digest / SHA-256

A binding to contents, not a signature or proof that observations are true, ensuring data integrity but not authenticity or proof of observation.

## valid versus qualification_authorized

Data consistency indicates that data meets specified criteria, but it does not grant permission to execute, qualify, or release the data, distinguishing between data integrity and authorization.
